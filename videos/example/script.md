# How a Black Hole Works — 30 seconds

1. **00–06** A black hole is a region where gravity is so strong that light cannot escape.
2. **06–14** The event horizon marks that boundary. It is not a solid surface you could stand on.
3. **14–22** Gas orbiting nearby can heat up and shine. The light comes from outside the black hole.
4. **22–30** Astronomers study nearby stars, glowing gas, and gravitational waves to learn about these invisible objects.
